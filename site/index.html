Pick your fantasy five from every possible bracket

A free Windows app for Counter-Strike fantasy leagues. It enumerates every way the current event can play out, works out what each player is worth across all of those outcomes, and finds the best five-player rosters with roles and boosters already assigned.

Download for Windows free · x64 installer

Updates install themselves. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice on first run: choose More info, then Run anyway. All versions

  • Every outcomeSingle and double elimination, group stages and Swiss: outcomes are enumerated, not sampled, wherever the format allows.
  • Every playerRating, win, role and booster points per round, with the fantasy game's padding and elimination rules applied.
  • Top 5 rostersBest average value, best single outcome, or most likely winner, sorted by score, cost or value.
